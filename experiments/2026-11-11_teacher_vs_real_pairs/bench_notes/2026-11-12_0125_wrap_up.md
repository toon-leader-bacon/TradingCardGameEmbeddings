## The rules as written in the Aim, applied mechanically

The verdict belongs to the PI and the human.

- **Speed rule** (Δ ≥ +0.02 at every first-half round): the first-half Δs are +0.014, +0.015, +0.010 and +0.006. All are positive with CIs excluding 0, but none reaches +0.02. None is ≤ −0.02, so the rule gives "no clear difference".
- **Quality rule** (final round): Δ = −0.003, CI excluding 0, inside the ±0.02 margin. The linear-cls guard is breached, though: T is 0.108 below R, against an allowed 0.02. So the rule gives "refuted".
- **Descriptive:** T never reaches R's final deck AUC (0.8673); T's best is 0.8661, at 60k steps. R needs about 40k steps to reach T's 20k-step AUC (0.860).

## Observations across runs

- T leads early and R catches up. The Δ shrinks every round after round 1 and changes sign at about 60k steps.
- T loses card-field information steadily (linear-cls 0.713 → 0.591, effective rank 53 → 33). R holds steady (0.71 → 0.70, rank 74 → 88). Both stay below the untrained head (0.739) and far below the frozen mean (0.927).
- T's own TEST loss bottoms out at rounds 2-3 (main_T bench note), the same rounds where its deck-AUC gain stalls.
- Repeat-run check: `main_R_attempt1` vs `main_R` round 7 gives Δ = 0.0000 exactly. Training is deterministic at a fixed seed here, so the bootstrap CIs contain no run-to-run noise. Seed-to-seed spread is unmeasured.
