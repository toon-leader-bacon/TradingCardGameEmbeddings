**Where the time goes**

- Mixed diet, steady:
  - Waiting for the batch takes 19% of summed step time at N=0 and 36% at N=4. In the median step it is only 0.8% and 1.4%: the waiting falls on a few dojos.
  - Forward + loss takes 54% and 42%.
  - On the training thread, serialization and cache lookups take about 11% at N=0.
  - Mods are never on the training thread. On the worker they took 27 s (N=0, task mods) and 115 s (N=4) in a whole run.
- MTG alone is bound by building batches:
  - The worker builds a 512-card batch in a median of 0.21 s (mtg_n0) and 0.19 s (mtg_n4).
  - The step takes 0.225 s and 0.247 s, and waits 69% and 73% of the time.
- The cost of N=4 is live encoding of variant texts on the worker:
  - mixed: 1,652 s for 305,513 texts, against 634 s for 157,971;
  - MTG: 335 s for 44,485 texts, against 0.
- The slowest dojos are the same in every mixed run:
  - `scryfall.card_type_mask`, `rarity_mask` and `colors_mask` take a median 2.1-2.3 s per step at batch cost 512, and 7-9 s at 2048, nearly all of it waiting (task-masked texts that the warm-up never sees).
  - Then the Pokemon and Hearthstone metric dojos.
  - Then `contrastive.mtg`, at 0.29-0.35 s, which has the slowest batch build of the contrastive dojos (about 0.5 ms per card).
