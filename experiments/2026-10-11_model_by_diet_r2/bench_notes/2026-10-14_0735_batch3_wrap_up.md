## Observations across runs

TEST losses only, one seed (pilot strength). They don't bear directly on
the Aim's bar, H1 or H2, which are judged on the per-card probe.

- **Model, same diet:** multi-card has the lower TEST median in every
  comparison, by more than 0.02 in all but one:
  - contrastive-only: 0.532 against 0.670;
  - metric-only: 0.936 against 0.997;
  - mixed, contrastive dojos: 0.662 against 0.745;
  - mixed, trained metric: 0.942 against 0.947 (under 0.02, no clear
    difference).
- **Diet, trained metric:** mixed reached about the same or better
  median than metric-only with half the metric steps:
  - multi-card: 0.942 mixed against 0.936 metric-only (no clear
    difference);
  - single-card: 0.947 mixed against 0.997 metric-only.
- **Diet, contrastive:** contrastive-only beats mixed on the contrastive
  dojos in both models (multi 0.532 against 0.662; single 0.670 against
  0.745), consistent with mixed giving them half the steps.
- **Held out:** no cell moved the held-out median below 1.08. The two
  mixed cells have the lowest held-out medians (1.083, 1.093); the
  single-objective cells sit at 1.11-1.15.
- **Still improving at the end:** `single_mixed` fell in every round on
  contrastive and trained metric, like the multi-card cells.
- **Stability:** every run finished; 6-12 fp16 overflow steps skipped per
  run, no NaNs.
- **Resources:** VRAM peaked at 5.6 GB or less of 16 GB in every cell,
  Python RAM at 4.2 GB or less, and free RAM never went below 16 GB. GPU
  compute busy medians were 57-76%, and Python CPU about 2.7 of 16
  threads throughout. The same headroom as batch 2.
