## Observations across runs

These are TEST losses only, from one seed (pilot strength). They don't
bear directly on the Aim's bar, H1 or H2, which are judged on the
per-card probe.

- **Multi-card kept improving to the end; single-card levelled off
  earlier.**
  - `multi_contrastive` and `multi_metric` fell in every round.
  - `single_contrastive` peaked in round 2.
  - `single_metric` was nearly flat after round 1 (1.004 to 0.997).
- **On the same diet, multi-card beats single-card on TEST.**
  - Contrastive: 0.532 against 0.670.
  - Metric: 0.936 against 0.997.
  - Both gaps are well beyond the 0.02 noise threshold. Contrastive
    TEST is in-context (sets of cards), where group attention is
    expected to help. That isn't what H2 is about: H2 is judged on
    per-card probes.
- **Mixed against single-objective, multi-card:**
  - Trained metric: mixed reached 0.942 with half the metric steps,
    against metric-only's 0.936. That is no clear difference (under
    0.02).
  - Contrastive: mixed reached 0.662 against contrastive-only's 0.532.
- **No diet moved the held-out dojos.** In every cell they stay at
  1.08-1.15 from round 0 to round 3, and almost none go below 1.0 (at
  most 1 of 13).
- **Stability:** all four runs were stable. Between 6 and 12 fp16 overflow
  steps were skipped per run, with no NaNs and no metric dojo above 5×
  (the batch's max was 2.43).
- **Resources** (`results/resources_by_cell.csv`):
  - VRAM peaked at 5.0 GB or less on the 16 GB card, and Python RAM at
    4.2 GB or less. Free system RAM never went below 16 GB.
  - GPU compute was busy a median of 76% in the metric-bearing cells,
    and 57% in `single_contrastive`.
  - Python CPU was flat at about 2.6-2.8 of 16 threads in every sampled
    run.
  - The same pattern held in all three fully sampled runs: a largely
    single-threaded host loop, idle GPU time, and most of the VRAM free.
    Larger `max_batch_cost`, or batch building off the main thread, are
    candidates for a throughput pilot after this experiment. Within it,
    every cell must keep `max_batch_cost` 256.
