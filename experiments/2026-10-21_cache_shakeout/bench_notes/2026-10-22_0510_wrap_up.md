## probe_nomods_b512 (no page of its own)

This is `cached_nomods` with `max_batch_cost` 512, 500 steps, and
`--no-binder-copy`. Its row in `results/run_summary.csv` gives:

- **Throughput:** 1,051 cards/s, against 965 in `cached_nomods`, so 9%
  more.
  - Mean step 0.467 s, against 0.255 s.
  - The round 0 training time for the same number of cards was 233 s,
    against 270 s.
- **GPU busy:** median 24.5%, against 20.8%.
- **Step shares:** batch wait 16%, backward + optimizer 35%,
  lookup 18%.
- **Binder check:** unchanged.
- **Memory:** peak torch allocated 4.5 GiB, peak process VRAM 6.0 GB.
- **Against the prediction** (PI Log, written before the run): about
  1,000 cards/s means per-card bound; above about 1,500 means fixed
  per-step overhead. The result is 1,051, which is per-card bound.

## Observations across runs

These are numbers set against the criteria the notebook wrote before
the runs. The verdicts are for the PI and the human.

- **H1 (mods).** In `cached`, live encoding was 82% of step time (the
  criterion is 30%). `cached_nomods` was 4.8× faster per card (965 vs
  202 cards/s; the criterion is 1.3×). `cached_seed1` reproduced
  `cached` within 2%.
- **H2 (TEST).** A cached TEST pass took 70–87 s, about 60–70 cached
  steps. The hypothesis threshold was 1,000 steps. With no mods, a pass
  costs about 300 steps. In `uncached`, a pass took 850 s.
- **H3 (deep copy).** Deep copies were 29% of TRAIN batch-building time
  in `cached` (the criterion is ≥10%). Skipping them left all 7 binders
  unchanged, in `cached_nocopy` and again in `probe_nomods_b512`. They
  also cost about 1,300 s of `build_dojos` (514 vs about 1,830 s) and
  about 30% of each TEST pass.
- **H4 (cache equivalence).** The two parts come out differently:
  - **Losses.** The median absolute normalized TEST-loss gap between
    `cached` and `uncached` was 0.032. The criterion was "no larger than
    `cached` vs `cached_nocopy`", and that pair's gap was 0.035. The
    pair was not bit-identical, so the 0.02 fallback does not apply.
    The free-form seed pair (`cached` vs `cached_seed1`) gap was 0.056.
  - **Speed.** Per step, `cached` was 1.21× faster than `uncached`; the
    criterion was ≥3×.
  - The fp16-storage confound stays open, but any effect it has is
    within the noise.
- **Where a run's time goes now** (r2 recipe, default mods, 2,000
  steps):
  1. Live encoding of mod-stamped cards: about 1.0 of 1.22 s per step.
  2. Startup: about 32 min, of which about 22 min is deep copies. The
     rest is calibration plus the deck-set digest (startup probe in the
     PI Log, 22:35).
  3. TEST: about 2.6 min per run.
  4. Batch building on the worker: hidden, at 0.6% wait.
- **With mods off,** the order changes:
  1. Startup becomes 73% of a run.
  2. The step is CPU-bound with the GPU idle. Backward + optimizer is
     about a third. Batch wait is 13–16%: cold reserve encodes of
     target-masked card-field texts, see the PI Log at 00:58.
  3. Doubling the batch gained only 9%.
- **One dojo is an outlier:**
  `seventeenlands_replay_data.attacker_blocker_combat_outcome`. Its step
  costs about 4× its siblings', with or without mods (4.5 s cached).
  Its texts take about 18 ms each to encode, against about 5 ms for
  other texts. Token lengths were not measured.
- **Noise:**
  - Contrastive TEST losses agree across runs to about 0.004–0.012.
  - Metric TEST losses vary by 0.04–0.08 at the median, and the p90
    reaches 0.6.
  - Timing reproduces within about 2%.
  - `build_dojos` varied between 1,813 and 1,857 s with copies on, and
    between 514 and 683 s with copies off. The pilot's 3,164 s was a
    cold start.
- **Memory:**
  - The cache costs about 4 GB of RAM and about 3.4 GB of VRAM (cached
    against uncached).
  - The RAM tier was set to 4,096 MiB and served 0 hits in every run.
    The VRAM tier never passed 87k of its 1.4M rows.
  - Lowest free RAM was 13.1 GB, and peak process VRAM was 7.6 GB
    (of 16 GB).
- **Data exposure** at the `cached` rate of 2,956 steps/h
  (`results/data_exposure_cached.csv`):
  - Saturated sets, repeated many times a day:
    - `contrastive*.pokemon` (159 decks, 237–719 passes per 24 h)
    - `contrastive.flesh_and_blood` (3,353 decks, 53 passes per 24 h)
    - the small card tables `spire_codex`, `sts_gg.card_*` and
      `dominiontabs`, each about 400–1,800 rows and about 25–30
      passes per 24 h
  - The large deck and game-log sets (`sts2_runs`, `isotropic`,
    `final_decks`, MTG and STS2 decks) take hundreds to thousands of
    hours per pass.
  - At the `cached_nomods` rate (14,100 steps/h), every rate is 4.8×
    higher (`results/data_exposure_cached_nomods.csv`).
