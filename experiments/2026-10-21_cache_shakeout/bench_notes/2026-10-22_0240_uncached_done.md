## First observations

- **With default mods on, the cache saves little on training steps
  (1.21×) but a lot on TEST (10–12×).** TEST texts carry no mods, so all
  of them are cacheable. Most training texts are not.
- **A live-encoded text is cheaper here than in `cached`** (5.3 vs
  6.6 ms). One explanation is that encoding a whole batch amortizes the
  encoder better than encoding the leftover subset after cache hits.
  This was not measured.
- **The RAM cost of the cache is about 4 GB.** The RAM tier is set to
  4,096 MiB and had no hits in any run so far.
